How do I cancel my subscription?
Your subscription renews automatically through Pesapal. After each successful payment, Pesapal emails you a link to manage or cancel it, sent to whichever address you used for card billing.

To cancel: open that email, tap the management link, verify with the one-time code (OTP) Pesapal sends you, then you'll land on a dashboard where you can pause or cancel. You can do this anytime, even right after subscribing.
Access billing support
Have a billing question, or can't find the Pesapal email? Head to the Saved tab at the bottom of the app and tap Contact in the footer — we're happy to help.
